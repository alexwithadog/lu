# Lu and its references

Lu's character and interface assets are original vector artwork. No Coucou or Mochi character artwork, sounds, or logos are included.

The application uses Electron (MIT) and its bundled Chromium, Node.js, and other components. Their licenses accompany the packaged runtime in `LICENSE`, `LICENSES.chromium.html`, and related runtime files. Development tools include Vite, TypeScript, and @electron/packager under their respective open-source licenses.

The separate `coucou/` research checkout is not part of Lu's packaged application and retains its own license and asset restrictions. Design and workflow research references include [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou), [CC Switch](https://github.com/farion1231/cc-switch), [Open Dots](https://github.com/Anil-matcha/open-dots), [Open Interpreter](https://github.com/openinterpreter/open-interpreter), and [Tauri](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri).

Lu does not bundle these projects' services or imply access to their hosted infrastructure. Optional AI services are configured by the user.
